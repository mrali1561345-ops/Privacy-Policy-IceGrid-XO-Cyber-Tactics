# Privacy Policy for IceGrid XO Cyber Tactics

**Last updated:** October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application **IceGrid XO: Cyber Tactics** ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through compliance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
IceGrid XO: Cyber Tactics is built as an offline-first puzzle game. We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.

## 2. Local Data Storage
All gameplay data, including wins, losses, draws, and setting preferences (such as sound and haptic toggles), are stored locally on your device only. Resetting the app data or uninstalling the App will permanently clear these statistics.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The application does not integrate third-party advertising frameworks, tracking SDKs, or analytics software that collect user data.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13 intentionally, nor do we knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
